📌 How to use this resource

Clinical-pathological models are best suited for initial risk stratification because they require only routine clinical and pathological data.

Bulk molecular signatures add biological insight by distinguishing early metastatic recurrence from late de novo recurrence.

AI and multimodal models leverage imaging or pathology to capture non-linear patterns, often achieving the highest cross-sectional predictive accuracy.

Single-cell/spatial models currently serve more as discovery platforms, with TIMES representing an early translational example.

Liquid biopsy shifts prediction from a single preoperative probability to longitudinal dynamic monitoring of molecular residual disease.
