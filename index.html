Clinical Time PointTissue profiling at primary / early recurrent disease

Evidence StageBiomarker discovery

📌 How to use this resource

Baseline risk stratification: Clinicopathological, molecular, imaging, and spatial approaches primarily estimate the probability of future recurrence, but they measure different biological processes and should not be ranked only by AUC or C-index.

Biological interpretation: Tumor-derived signatures may reflect dissemination or aggressive clones, adjacent-liver signatures may capture field-effect carcinogenesis, and single-cell/spatial studies resolve cellular states and local immune ecosystems.

Dynamic MRD monitoring: Postoperative ctDNA occupies a distinct clinical position because it aims to detect molecular residual disease that is already present and can be followed longitudinally.

Evidence maturity: Performance should be interpreted together with calibration, external validation, cross-center reproducibility, assay standardization, and whether model-guided management has demonstrated clinical benefit.

Scope: This portal is a curated, continuously updated companion resource. Evidence-stage labels are descriptive summaries of reported validation maturity and do not constitute a formal risk-of-bias reassessment.
